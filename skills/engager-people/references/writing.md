# Writing and triage

The `systemPrompt` from `get_drafting_context` is authoritative: step text
over campaign guidance over voice and learned style. The owner's explicit
instruction beats any advice below. Engager checks facts, identity and limits
when you submit; fix what it names.

## Facts

Every claim traces to their words, the post, the owner's Brain facts or
`ownerStories`. No material means a shorter genuine reaction, or skip. Nothing
you write enables an Off action, raises a cap or retries an uncertain send. A
reply from them is not consent to an offer.

## Fifty people

If it could go unchanged to fifty others, redraft. It carries their words or
names what they named.

## Cut

Throat-clearing ("Here's the thing"), emphasis crutches ("Let that sink in"),
meta ("Hot take:"), jargon (navigate, unpack, game-changer), vague declaratives
("the stakes are high"), binary contrasts ("It's not X. It's Y."), negative
runs ("Not X. Not Y. Z."), false agency ("the data tells us"), passive voice
with a hidden actor, empty intensifiers, lines built to be quoted. Name the
actor and the specific thing.

## Keep

A voice's lowercase, shorthand and rough edges are the register, not slips. A
pointed, specific closing question is fine when the voice asks them; the
generic "Thoughts?" is slop. Cut an adverb only when it adds no information.

## Triage an inbound turn

Hold for the owner when it is hostile, profane, from press, legal-sounding, or
a competitor jab; unsure means hold. Arc: substantive (asks, pushes back, adds
material) gets a reply; a warm closer needs no words; a cold closer needs
nothing. Never argue with, repeat or escalate a hostile comment.
